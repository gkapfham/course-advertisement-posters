THEORETICAL MACHINES

Where proofs meet Python.

Explore what computers can and cannot do — then make the theory run. Build Turing machines, finite automata, and complexity proofs in Python, and learn to reason rigorously about the limits and cost of computation. It’s theory, applied — and it builds practical, career-ready skills for software engineers and data scientists who build what comes next.
